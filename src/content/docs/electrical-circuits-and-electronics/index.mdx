Every digital system is ultimately an electrical circuit. This course covers circuit analysis (Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws, nodal and mesh analysis, network theorems, AC circuits) and the semiconductor devices behind logic gates and amplifiers: diodes, transistors and op-amps. It leads into [Digital Logic Design](/digital-logic-design/).

## Standard textbooks

- Robert L. Boylestad: *Introductory Circuit Analysis*
- Charles K. Alexander & Matthew N. O. Sadiku: *Fundamentals of Electric Circuits*
- Robert L. Boylestad & Louis Nashelsky: *Electronic Devices and Circuit Theory*
