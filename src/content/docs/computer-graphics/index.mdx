Computer graphics is about turning geometric descriptions into pixels. This course follows the classic pipeline: how displays and frame buffers work, how lines, circles and polygons are drawn and clipped, how objects are moved and viewed with matrix transformations, how 3D scenes are projected onto a 2D screen, and how hidden surfaces are removed and surfaces shaded. It ends with Bézier and B-spline curves. Exam questions are often step-by-step traces (Bresenham, midpoint circle, Cohen–Sutherland, composite transformations), so each lesson includes a worked trace.
