An operating system shares one machine's CPU, memory and disks among many programs, safely and efficiently. Scheduling, synchronization, deadlocks and paging are among the most-asked topics in lecturer vivas and admission tests, and most exam questions are worked calculations you can practise.

## Standard textbooks

- Silberschatz, Galvin & Gagne: *Operating System Concepts* (the "dinosaur book")
- Andrew S. Tanenbaum & Herbert Bos: *Modern Operating Systems*
- Remzi & Andrea Arpaci-Dusseau: *Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces* (free online)
