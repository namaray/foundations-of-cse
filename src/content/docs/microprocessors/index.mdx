This course studies one real processor in detail, the Intel **8086**, the ancestor of every x86 chip, and how it connects to memory and peripheral devices. Physical address calculation, addressing modes, short assembly programs, interrupt vectors and 8255 control words are the classic exam questions. It applies [Computer Architecture](/computer-architecture/) to concrete hardware.
