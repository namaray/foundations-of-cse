Computer architecture is the design of the processor and its memory system: what instructions a CPU understands, how the hardware carries them out, and how to make that fast. Performance equations, addressing modes, pipeline hazards and cache address breakdowns are the standard exam calculations. It builds on [Digital Logic Design](/digital-logic-design/).

## Standard textbooks

- David A. Patterson & John L. Hennessy: *Computer Organization and Design* (RISC-V or MIPS edition)
- John L. Hennessy & David A. Patterson: *Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach*
- William Stallings: *Computer Organization and Architecture*
- M. Morris Mano: *Computer System Architecture*
