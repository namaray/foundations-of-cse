Discrete mathematics studies things you can count: statements that are true or false, sets, integers and graphs. It is the language behind algorithms, databases, cryptography and the theory of computation, and it appears in almost every admission test.

- Seymour Lipschutz & Marc Lipson: *Discrete Mathematics* (Schaum’s Outline)
