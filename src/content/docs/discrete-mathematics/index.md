Logic, proofs, sets, counting, recurrences, graphs and number theory: the mathematical language of computer science.

## Topics to master

- [ ] Propositional and predicate logic
- [ ] Proof techniques: direct, contradiction, induction
- [ ] Sets, relations and functions
- [ ] Counting: permutations, combinations, pigeonhole principle, inclusion–exclusion
- [ ] Recurrence relations and generating functions
- [ ] Graph theory: paths, connectivity, Euler and Hamilton circuits, coloring
- [ ] Trees and spanning trees
- [ ] Boolean algebra
- [ ] Number theory: divisibility, GCD, modular arithmetic, primes
- [ ] Partial orders and lattices

## Standard textbooks

- Kenneth H. Rosen: *Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications*
- Seymour Lipschutz: *Discrete Mathematics* (Schaum’s Outline)

## Key formula

Inclusion–exclusion for two sets:

$$
|A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|
$$
