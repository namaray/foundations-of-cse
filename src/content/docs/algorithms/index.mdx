Every algorithm raises three questions: *is it correct?*, *how fast is it?*, and *can we do better?* This course answers them for the algorithms that come up most in lecturer vivas and master's admission tests. Read [Data Structures](/data-structures/) first.

<CourseRoadmap course="algorithms" />

## How each lesson works

1. **Start with the top box.** It lists what you'll be able to do and gives the key idea in a few sentences.
2. **Work through the sections in order.** Each one covers a single idea with an example you can follow on paper. Boxes marked *Go deeper* are optional.
3. **Finish with the summary and *Check yourself*.** If you can answer the questions without peeking, mark the lesson as done.

Your progress is saved in this browser only.

## Standard textbooks

- Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest & Stein: *Introduction to Algorithms* (CLRS)
- Jon Kleinberg & Éva Tardos: *Algorithm Design*
- Ellis Horowitz, Sartaj Sahni & Sanguthevar Rajasekaran: *Fundamentals of Computer Algorithms*
