## Typical stages

- [ ] Written test on core CSE subjects
- [ ] Viva board with faculty members
- [ ] Demo class on a chosen or assigned topic
- [ ] Discussion of research interests and publications

## Preparing a demo class

- Pick a topic you can teach clearly in 15–20 minutes, such as Dijkstra's algorithm, deadlocks or normalization.
- Open with motivation: why the topic matters.
- Teach with one worked example on the board.
- End with a short question to check understanding.
