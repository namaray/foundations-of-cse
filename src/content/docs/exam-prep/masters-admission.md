## Before you start

- [ ] Collect the official syllabus and past papers for each university you are applying to
- [ ] Note the exam format: MCQ, written, or both, and whether there is negative marking
- [ ] Note the time per question

## High-priority topics

Most CSE admission tests weigh these subjects heavily:

- Data Structures and Algorithms
- Discrete Mathematics
- Theory of Computation
- Operating Systems
- Computer Networks
- Database Systems
- Computer Architecture and Digital Logic

## Practice strategy

- Revise one course per week, then take a timed mixed test.
- Keep an error log: every wrong answer goes on the relevant course page as a note.
