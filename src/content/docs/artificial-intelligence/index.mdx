Artificial intelligence builds programs that act rationally: they perceive their environment, reason about it and choose good actions. This course covers the classical core: agents, search (uninformed, heuristic, adversarial), constraint satisfaction, logic, reasoning under uncertainty and fuzzy logic. Learning from data is covered in [Machine Learning](/machine-learning/).
