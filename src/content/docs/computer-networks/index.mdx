Networking is organised in layers, and this course climbs them one at a time: signals on a wire, frames between neighbours, packets across the Internet, reliable streams between programs, and finally the applications you use. Subnetting, sliding windows, CSMA/CD and TCP congestion control are the calculation-heavy favourites in exams.
