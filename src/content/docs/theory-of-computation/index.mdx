Theory of computation asks what machines can compute at all, and with how much memory. It climbs a ladder of machine models (finite automata, pushdown automata, Turing machines), each matched to a class of languages, then shows that some problems no machine can ever solve. Admission tests love DFA design, regular expressions, the pumping lemma and the Chomsky hierarchy.

## Standard textbooks

- Michael Sipser: *Introduction to the Theory of Computation*

- Peter Linz: *An Introduction to Formal Languages and Automata*
